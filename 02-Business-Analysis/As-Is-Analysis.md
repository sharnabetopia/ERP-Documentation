# As-Is Process Analysis

Document status: **Template with hypotheses. Not validated.**
No customer or current-state evidence has been supplied.
The content below states the typical pre-ERP situation and the questions to ask.
It must not be quoted as fact.
Workshops needed: see OQ-012, OQ-029.

## 1. Method

1. Interview process owners per domain.
2. Walk through one real document of each type from start to finish.
3. Record tools, hand-offs, approvals, delays, errors and controls.
4. Capture volumes and cycle times.
5. Replace each hypothesis below with evidence or remove it.

## 2. Hypothesised current state by process

| Process | Typical current practice (hypothesis) | Typical pain | Evidence needed | Related problem |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Procure-to-Pay | Requests by email or paper. POs in spreadsheets. Invoices matched by hand. | Duplicate payments, slow approvals. | Count of POs per month, duplicate incidents. | PRB-02, PRB-04 |
| Order-to-Cash | Quotes in documents. Orders in a sales tool or sheet. Invoices from accounting software. | Re-keying, late invoices. | Order to invoice lead time. | PRB-01 |
| Record-to-Report | Ledger in accounting software with manual consolidation. | Slow close, adjustments. | Days to close. | PRB-05 |
| Inventory | Counts in spreadsheets, stock not tied to ledger. | Stock-outs, shrinkage. | Stock accuracy rate. | PRB-03 |
| Hire-to-Retire | HR files and attendance sheets. Payroll by spreadsheet. | Errors, privacy exposure. | Payroll error rate. | PRB-06 |
| Approvals | Email, chat, signatures. | No audit record. | Sample of approvals. | PRB-02, PRB-07 |
| Reporting | Manual exports. | Stale, inconsistent figures. | List of recurring reports. | PRB-01 |
